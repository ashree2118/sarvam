# Brand voice

Short, structured, confident. Rooted, never nostalgic. Proud, never loud.

## 1. Principles (real Sarvam lines)
| Principle | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| Short declaratives | "India Can." · "Not a handoff. A partnership." · "Expressive, accurate, fast." |
| National, never jingoistic | "The AI Platform India Builds On." · "Sovereign by design." · "Built for India." |
| Human at the core | "Who gets to be understood?" · "In your own words." |
| Show, don't claim | "Don't take our word for it. Listen." · "Now write your own." |
| Developer-direct | "Add Text to Speech to your app in minutes." · "One line to install." |
| Proof over adjectives | "10B+ tokens served · <100ms median latency · 99.9% uptime SLA." · "From ₹3 a minute." |
| Invitation | "Begin here." · "Ask Sarvam." |

## 2. Headline patterns
Two-beat declarative ("One model. Many experiences.") · claim + qualifier ("Built for the depth of Indian speech.") · open question, campaign only ("Who gets to be understood?") · imperative invitation ("Now write your own.") · positioning line ("The AI Platform India Builds On."). Eight words at most; if longer, cut the adjectives.

## 3. Mechanics
- Sentence case; product names keep their capitals. **No "!"** and no hype: *revolutionary, magic, game-changing, unleash, supercharge, cutting-edge, seamless, next-gen*.
- Indian English spelling (colour, centre, optimisation, programme). ₹ for prices; Indian grouping where it helps (₹1,00,000, "1 lakh"); otherwise "10B+", "<100ms", "22 languages". Spell out one to nine in prose.
- Indian names and places in examples (Arjun, Aparna, Rahul; Bengaluru, Pune, Guwahati). Indic scripts native; code-mixed lines welcome.
- Keep the last two words of a headline together with `&nbsp;`. One idea per sentence; prefer verbs to nouns.

## 4. Naming
- "Sarvam AI" on first mention, then "Sarvam": "India's full-stack sovereign AI platform". Legal entity in footers: Axonwise Private Limited. Tagline: AI for all from India.
- **Products, exactly as published:** Indus · Indus Cowork · Samvaad (Voice Agents) · Bulbul (Text to Speech; current model *Bulbul V4*, variant *Flash*) · Saaras (Speech to Text) · Mayura (Translation) · Parse · Sarvam-105B · Sarvam Vision · Sarvam Prism · Sarvam Edge · Content Studio · Doc Agents · Work Agents · Coding Agents · Token Factory. Never abbreviate them or use model names as app titles.
- Partners say "powered by Sarvam AI" or "built with Sarvam AI's speech-to-text API".

## 5. Copy length
| Format | Kicker | Headline | Support | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Banner | ≤ 4 words | ≤ 8 words, 2 lines | ≤ 12 words, or 2–3 stats | Not on the image (one pill allowed on launch banners) |
| Landing hero | Eyebrow ≤ 5 words | ≤ 8 words | 1 sentence, ≤ 22 words | 2 buttons, 1–3 words each |
| Section | Overline ≤ 4 words | ≤ 8 words | 1–2 sentences | Optional secondary |
| Card | — | ≤ 6 words | ≤ 25 words | Optional text link |
| Band | — | ≤ 10 words | 1 sentence | 1 button |
